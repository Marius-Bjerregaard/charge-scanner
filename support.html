Frequently asked questions

No chargers are found. Check that Charge Scanner has access to your location (iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services) and that you are online. Try raising the maximum distance or lowering the minimum power in the app's settings, or turn more providers back on.

A charger shows as available, but it is busy. Availability comes from the charging providers and can be a few minutes behind.

"The charger service is temporarily busy". The charger data service limits how many searches can be made. Wait a little and try again.

Navigation has no voice. Turn on Voice guidance in Settings > Navigation, and check the volume and the silent switch.

How do I connect my car? In Settings > Extras, enter your Open Vehicle Monitoring System (OVMS) server or MQTT broker, vehicle ID and password, and tap Test connection.

How do I use the AI assistant? In Settings > Extras, choose Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini and paste an API key from that provider. Usage is billed by the provider to your own account.
