Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Charge Scanner has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. The developer does not run any server and does not collect or store data about you.

Location

The app uses your location to find chargers near you and to guide you to them. To do this, coordinates are sent to:

  • TomTom – to search for chargers and their live availability. TomTom privacy policy
  • Apple – for maps, address search and routes (MapKit). Apple privacy policy

Addresses you search for and your home address are stored only on your device.

Optional: car connection (OVMS)

If you connect a car, the app logs in to the OVMS server or MQTT broker you enter and reads battery level, range and a few other figures. Your login is stored in the iOS keychain on your device and is only sent to that server.

Optional: AI assistant

If you add an API key and ask the assistant, the app sends the search location (coordinates and, if you searched for one, the address), your search filters and the names of chargers already shown to the provider you chose: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT) or Google (Gemini). That provider's privacy policy applies. The API key is stored in the iOS keychain on your device and is only sent to that provider.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
